# jazz-inbox

Raw material for the Jazz room (a third door beside Music and Ambient), until
it is built. Nothing here is committed except this file.

    jazz-inbox/
      kissa.png          the room, photoreal, 16:9, at least 1920 wide
      kissa.mp4          optional: real footage of the rain, framed like the still
      music/             the programme — instrumental only, one channel

## The room

One photograph, the night kissa: records, speakers, siphons, rain on the
window. No bottles, no watermark, no album cover that copies a real one.
Prefer a person at the counter, left of centre — on a phone the headline
covers the bottom half, so the coffee cup there is atmosphere, not subject.

## The music

One channel, no speed switch: everyone in the room hears the same piece.
60–95 bpm; piano trio, brushes, upright bass, soft guitar, vibraphone; horns
only now and then. No vocals or scat, no bebop, no drum solos.

Only original or properly licensed pieces — a standard like "Autumn Leaves" is
still under copyright in a new recording. Write each file's source and licence
below as it arrives.
